---
name: Tone and style
summary: Cozy-dark folk tale: small stakes, warm people, eerie places; plain spoken dialogue.
tags: tone, style guide
---
- Dialogue is short and plain-spoken; villagers talk like farmers, not poets.
- Humor is dry and gentle. No modern slang, no pop-culture references.
- The undead are eerie and sad rather than gory.
- Names are short and rural-English sounding (Aren, Tamsin, Brookvale).
